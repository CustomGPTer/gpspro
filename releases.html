Release notes

What changed in each version of GPS Pro. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.1 24 November 2026Latest

  • Every page now scrolls far enough for its last card to sit clear of the bottom bar, whatever navigation buttons or gestures your phone uses.
  • Cards and tiles are a little more solid, so the contour lines behind them show through less.

Version 1.0.0 24 November 2026

  • First release. 23 GPS tools for Android, with every online extra off until you switch it on.
  • Your position in seven formats including OS grid references, compass with target bearing, speedometer with head-up display, satellites, level, coordinate converter, GPS time, sensors and magnetometer.
  • Street and Topo maps with hill shading and contour lines, offline areas, place and postcode search, Map Tools and saved places with photos.
  • Track recorder with auto-pause, splits, charts and GPX and KML, altimeter, step tracker, GPS alarm, geofences shared by link or QR code, and a route optimiser for up to 60 stops.
  • Weather with Met Office warnings, a seven-day forecast, the UK air quality index with pollen, and sun and moon times.
  • Emergency screen, flashlight with SOS and Morse, position and shortcut widgets, and an optional GPS status notification.
  • Backup and restore in one file, with an optional password.
  • Dark, black, light and night red themes with night mode sliders, and every unit you need.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.1 is called GPSPro.apk. It is 27.1 MB (27,086,676 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

7e4d5aa211d02aacf94abf40752177a3914d29ce17f9d8b9cbd16fd3515a119a

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile GPSPro.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
