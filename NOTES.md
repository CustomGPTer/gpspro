The first release of GPS Pro: 23 GPS tools for Android that keep your position on your phone.

- Your position in seven formats including OS grid references, a compass with target bearing, speedometer with head-up display, satellites, level, coordinate converter, GPS time, sensors and magnetometer
- Street and Topo maps with contour lines, offline areas, place and postcode search, Map Tools and saved places with photos
- Track recorder with auto-pause, splits, charts and GPX and KML, altimeter, step tracker, GPS alarm, geofences and a route optimiser for up to 60 stops
- Weather with Met Office warnings, a seven-day forecast, the UK air quality index with pollen, and sun and moon times
- Emergency screen, flashlight with SOS and Morse, widgets and an optional GPS status notification
- Backup and restore in one file, with an optional password
- No adverts, no account, and every online extra off until you switch it on

Needs Android 10 or later. Download `GPSPro.apk` below and open it to install.
