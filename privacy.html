Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026. This policy covers the GPS Pro app for Android (package uk.jj.gpspro) and this website. GPS Pro is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Your position, places, photos, tracks, geofences and settings stay on your phone. They are never uploaded to the developer.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • Each online extra is off until you switch it on, says what it sends, and is listed in the Network log every time it connects.

What stays on your phone

GPS Pro keeps your saved places and their photos, recorded tracks, geofences, route stops, daily step counts, downloaded map areas, the last weather report, emergency contacts, your medical note and your settings in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for GPS Pro, so none of this is copied to Google. Photos added to places have their hidden details, such as where and when they were taken, removed.

Like any website, these services see your phone's internet address when it connects. Each is run by its own organisation under its own policy. Offline mode in Settings, Privacy turns every extra off at once.

GPS Pro does not ask for your contacts, calls, messages, camera, microphone or storage. Emergency contacts are picked one at a time with Android's own picker, and calls and texts are made by your own phone and messages apps.

Files you share or save

Tracks, places, backups and emergency messages leave the phone only when you share or save them, to the app or folder you choose. A backup can be sealed with your password using AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your password with PBKDF2; without the password nobody, including the developer, can open it.

Links to this website

Settings, About GPS Pro has Website, Newer version? and More apps. Each opens this site in your browser.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete any place, track, geofence, map area or log entry in the app, and uninstalling GPS Pro deletes everything it holds.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands on your phone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the app list. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Children

GPS Pro collects nothing from anyone. Younger users should ask a parent before switching on the online extras.
