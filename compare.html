More tools, and nothing collected

Two of the best-known GPS toolbox apps on Google Play sell upgrades inside the app and say they collect data. GPS Pro is free in full and keeps everything on your phone. This page shows only the rows where GPS Pro does more.

What GPS Pro does not do

GPS Pro has no smartwatch app, no weather map, no find-my-phone tracking and no turn-by-turn directions. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where they do more

GPS Tools has a Wear OS app, a weather map and lost-phone tracking. GPS Status & Toolbox can refresh the phone's GPS assistance data for a faster first fix. Both install and update from Google Play.
